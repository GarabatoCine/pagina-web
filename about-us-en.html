David David, Colombian Film Director and Writer

We create cinema from the Colombian Caribbean, where drama, fiction, and surrealism intersect to craft profoundly human stories with a unique identity and a global vocation.

DAVID DAVID

A filmmaker from Barranquilla and founder of Garabato Cine. His work navigates between dramatic fiction and touches of surrealism, framed within the vibrant context of the Colombian Caribbean. His series,  Colorful Life, is the only Colombian production ever awarded at Series Mania, where it received the Jury Special Prize. His feature film debut,  The Border, has been recognized at top-tier film festivals. A six-time winner of the Colombian Film Development Fund (FDC), his cinema is defined by a clear auteur voice, deeply rooted in local identity with a global reach.

CAROLINA DAVID

Executive Producer at Garabato Cine, where she leads the company's project development, resource management,and institutional growth. In her role, she brings together strategic and creative vision to strengthen the company's growth, consolidate its presence in international audiovisual spaces, and expand opportunities for the development, financing, and circulation of its projects. She holds a degree in Industrial Engineering and has a background in organizational consulting, which complements her experience in audiovisual management and production with a strategic and holistic approach.
